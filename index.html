QR Card Studio

Build a digital business card, style its QR code, and download a lock-screen image, a printable card and a contact file. Your details are stored inside the QR link, so there is no account or database.

Example details are filled in. Replace them with yours, or start blank.

Show images on the scanned card page (optional public image links)

A QR code is too small to carry pictures. Uploaded images appear on your downloads and the downloadable card page. To show them on the page people get when they scan, paste a public link to each image (for example the logo address from your website).

QR code
What happens when someone scans it

Upload this one file once to free hosting (GitHub Pages, Netlify or your own website) and paste its address here. Your details are packed into the QR itself, not stored on that site. If the site ever goes down, upload the same file to the same address and every printed code works again. For a code that never needs a website, choose Save my contact.

Add your card page address before you print or share. Until then the code points to a placeholder.
